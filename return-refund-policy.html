Return & Refund Policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026

1. Return eligibility

Return eligibility depends on the product, its condition and the return terms shown for the order or product. Some products may be non-returnable where clearly stated before purchase.

2. Return conditions

Eligible items should generally be unused, in saleable condition and accompanied by applicable original packaging, tags and accessories. Additional product-specific conditions may apply.

3. Requesting a return

Submit a return request through the available order/account support flow within the applicable return period. Provide the order details and the reason for the request. Supporting photos or other information may be requested.

4. Inspection

Returned items may be inspected before a return or refund is approved. Approval is subject to the applicable product and order conditions.

5. Refunds

Once an eligible refund is approved, the refund method and processing time depend on the original payment method and applicable payment or banking processes. Where supported, an eligible refund may be credited to the ApnaStore wallet.

6. Wrong, damaged or defective items

If you receive a wrong, damaged or defective item, contact support promptly with your order details and supporting evidence where available so the issue can be reviewed.

7. Shipping charges

Whether original or return shipping charges are refundable depends on the reason for the return and the terms applicable to the order.

8. Contact

For return or refund questions, contact Withapnastore@gmail.com.

9. Related policies

See our Shipping & Delivery Policy and Cancellation Policy.
