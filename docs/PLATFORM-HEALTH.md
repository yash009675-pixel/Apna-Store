# Phase 82 — Platform Health

Free-plan platform health foundation.

- Health-check registry with healthy/degraded/unhealthy/unknown states
- Metrics, units and structured diagnostic details
- Admin-only health recording and history
- 24-hour health summary
- Latest status per monitored check
- Audit logging
- Direct table access revoked
- No paid monitoring service introduced

External uptime/SLA certification is not claimed; final production health verification remains part of later QA/security work.
