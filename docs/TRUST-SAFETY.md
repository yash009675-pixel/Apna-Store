# Phase 80 — Trust & Safety

Free-plan trust and safety foundation.

- Customer-authenticated reporting for fraud, abuse, counterfeit and safety concerns
- Reports can target products, orders, reviews, profiles, sellers or other entities
- Priority levels: low, normal, high, urgent
- Admin-only review and status management
- Audit logging for report creation and status changes
- Direct table access revoked; protected RPCs enforce authorization
- No paid moderation or trust-and-safety service introduced

Final moderation policy, abuse testing and production QA remain part of later QA/security phases.
