Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026

This policy explains what personal data ApnaStore may collect, why it is used, how it may be shared with service providers, and the choices available to you.

1. Information we may collect

  • Account details such as name and email address.
  • Delivery and contact details needed to fulfil an order.
  • Order, cart, wishlist, membership, rewards and support-ticket information.
  • Newsletter or communication preferences when you choose to subscribe.
  • Technical information such as browser/device information, approximate usage information and security logs.

2. How we use information

We may use personal data to create and manage accounts, process and deliver orders, provide customer support, manage returns and refunds, operate memberships and rewards, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain security, improve the website, and send communications you have requested or are otherwise permitted to receive.

3. Payments

Where third-party payment processing is used, payment details may be handled directly by the payment provider according to its security and privacy practices. ApnaStore does not need to store full card credentials to operate the shopping service.

4. Service providers

We may use trusted service providers for authentication, hosting/database services, email communications, payment processing, delivery/logistics, analytics and other functions required to operate the service. They receive only information reasonably required for their service and are expected to protect it according to applicable requirements.

5. Cookies and local storage

The website may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, maintain shopping functionality, improve performance and support security. You can manage browser-level controls, although disabling some technologies may affect website functionality.

6. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal data. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so please use a strong password and contact us promptly if you suspect unauthorized account activity.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to access, correction, updating, withdrawal of consent, grievance handling and deletion of personal data, subject to lawful exceptions and retention requirements. Requests can be made using the contact details below. India’s Digital Personal Data Protection framework sets requirements around notice, consent and handling of personal data.

8. Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including order records, legal obligations, dispute resolution, security and accounting requirements. When information is no longer required, it may be deleted or anonymized where appropriate.

9. Children

ApnaStore is intended for users who can lawfully enter into transactions. If personal data relating to a child is provided without the required authorization, please contact us so we can review and take appropriate action under applicable law.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, practices or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be published here with a revised effective date.

11. Contact and privacy requests

For privacy questions, correction requests, consent-related requests or grievances, contact Withapnastore@gmail.com.
