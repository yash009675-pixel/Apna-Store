Shipping & Delivery Policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026

This policy explains how ApnaStore processes orders, handles delivery, and provides shipping information.

1. Order processing

Orders are processed after order confirmation and successful payment authorization, where applicable. Processing time may vary depending on the product, availability, and operational requirements.

2. Delivery

Estimated delivery information, where available, is shown during the shopping or order process. Delivery dates are estimates and may change because of courier delays, weather, public holidays, service disruptions, or an incomplete address.

3. Shipping charges

Applicable shipping charges, if any, are displayed before you confirm your order. Any free-shipping offer is subject to the terms shown with that offer.

4. Tracking

Where courier tracking is available, tracking information may be provided through your order or account experience. Tracking availability depends on the courier and shipment status.

5. Address and failed delivery

Please provide a complete and accurate delivery address and contact details. Additional delivery attempts or charges may apply when a shipment cannot be delivered because of an incorrect address, recipient unavailability, or other customer-related reasons.

6. Damaged or tampered packages

If a package appears damaged or tampered with, please contact ApnaStore Support as soon as possible and provide your order details and supporting photos, where available.

7. Contact

For shipping-related questions, contact Withapnastore@gmail.com.

8. Related policies

Please also review our Return & Refund Policy and Cancellation Policy.
