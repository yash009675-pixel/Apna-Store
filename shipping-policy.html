Shipping & Delivery Policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026

1. Order processing

Orders are processed after order confirmation and successful payment authorization where applicable. Processing time may vary by product, availability and operational requirements.

2. Delivery

Estimated delivery information, where available, is shown during the shopping or order process. Delivery dates are estimates and may change because of courier delays, weather, public holidays, service disruptions or an incomplete address.

3. Shipping charges

Applicable shipping charges, if any, will be displayed before order confirmation. Any free-shipping offer is subject to the terms shown with that offer.

4. Tracking

Where courier tracking is available, tracking information may be provided through the order/account experience. Tracking availability depends on the courier and shipment status.

5. Address and failed delivery

Please provide a complete and accurate delivery address and contact details. Additional delivery attempts or charges may apply where a shipment cannot be delivered because of an incorrect address, recipient unavailability or other customer-related reasons.

6. Damaged or tampered package

If a package appears damaged or tampered with, please contact ApnaStore support as soon as possible and provide order details and supporting photos where available.

7. Contact

For shipping questions, contact Withapnastore@gmail.com.

8. Related policies

See our Return & Refund Policy and Cancellation Policy.
