Grievance & Consumer Support

Effective date: 30 September 2026

We want customers to have a clear way to raise concerns about orders, delivery, returns, refunds, accounts, or other ApnaStore services.

1. Contact Support

For order, delivery, return, refund, account, or other website concerns, customers can use the ApnaStore Support flow or contact Withapnastore@gmail.com.

2. What to include

Please include your name, registered email where applicable, order ID or other relevant reference, a clear description of the issue, and supporting documents or images when useful.

3. Ticket handling

Where the Support system is used, a support ticket can be used to keep the conversation and responses together. Resolution depends on the nature of the issue and any information needed to investigate it.

4. Escalation

If an issue remains unresolved, reply to the existing support conversation or contact the Support email with the relevant ticket or order reference so it can be reviewed.

5. Privacy

Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive personal information. Personal data submitted for Support is handled according to our Privacy Policy.

6. Related policies

Please also review our Terms & Conditions, Shipping & Delivery Policy, and Return & Refund Policy.
