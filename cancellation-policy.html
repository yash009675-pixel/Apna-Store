Cancellation Policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026

1. Customer cancellation

An order may be cancelled when the order is in a status that permits cancellation in the ApnaStore order system. Once an order has progressed to dispatch or another non-cancellable stage, cancellation may no longer be available.

2. Prepaid orders

For an eligible prepaid cancellation, any approved refund will be processed according to the applicable payment method and refund process.

3. Cash on Delivery orders

COD orders may be cancelled only while the order is in an eligible cancellation status. After dispatch, cancellation may no longer be available.

4. Business-initiated cancellation

ApnaStore may cancel an order when necessary, including where a product is unavailable, an order cannot be fulfilled, payment or fraud checks are not completed, or delivery is not reasonably possible.

5. Refund after cancellation

Where a cancelled order is eligible for a refund, the applicable amount will be processed using the supported refund method for that order.

6. Contact

For cancellation questions, contact Withapnastore@gmail.com.

7. Related policies

See our Return & Refund Policy and Shipping & Delivery Policy.
